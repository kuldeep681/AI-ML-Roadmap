# Frequently Asked Questions

## Why FastAPI instead of Flask?

FastAPI is widely used for Machine Learning and AI APIs due to automatic documentation, validation and high performance.

---

## Why XGBoost before Deep Learning?

Understanding classical Machine Learning builds a strong foundation before neural networks.

---

## Why PyTorch instead of TensorFlow?

PyTorch is the dominant framework for modern AI research, Hugging Face and LLM development.

---

## Why FAISS before ChromaDB?

FAISS helps understand vector search fundamentals before using persistent vector databases.

---

## Why LangGraph after RAG?

Understanding retrieval pipelines first makes AI Agent workflows easier to understand.

---

## Do I need Kubernetes?

Basic Kubernetes knowledge is enough for most entry-level AI roles.

---

## Should I buy certificates?

No.

Auditing the courses is enough.

Projects matter more than certificates.

---

## Is this roadmap enough to get a job?

Completing the roadmap, building the projects and practicing DSA will prepare you well for entry-level AI and ML roles.
